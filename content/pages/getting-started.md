Before you change how you study, it helps to see clearly how you study *now*.
Take the short quiz below. Answer honestly — this is for you, not a grade. You'll
get a ranked list of habits to work on, with links into the lessons and the
Practice app. You can open any lesson or practice session from there — you are
not locked into one concept at a time.

## Where almost everyone should begin

Whatever your results, you can start anywhere. A reliable long-term path is to
work through **[Learning Materials](learning-materials.html)** — the first three
lessons reset foundations and mindset — but you may jump to later lessons that
match your quiz results. Practice those days (or any others) in the
**[Practice app](practice.html)**; every session is open, and the calendar
records the days you actually practice.

One commitment to make now: decide *how many focused minutes a day* you can
realistically protect. Even **15** is enough to start.
