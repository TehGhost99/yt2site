get a ranked list of habits to work on, with links into the lessons and the

## Where almost everyone should begin

Whatever your results, the most reliable long-term path is still to work through
**[Learning Materials](learning-materials.html)** in order. The first three lessons
reset foundations and mindset; everything else builds on them. Use your quiz
results to know which later lessons need extra attention — and practice those
days in the **[Practice app](practice.html)**.

One commitment to make now: decide *how many focused minutes a day* you can
realistically protect. Even **15** is enough to start.
