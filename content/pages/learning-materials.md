# Learning Materials
# Materiales de aprendizaje

**EN:** Work top to bottom. Lessons 1–12 build your first ~500-word foundation. Lessons 13–15 go deeper.

**ES:** Trabaja de arriba abajo. Las lecciones 1–12 construyen ~500 palabras. Las 13–15 profundizan.

## Part 1 — Core foundation / Base

1. **[Greetings & Sounds](greetings-sounds.html)** — Sounds, hola, courtesy, and first conversations.
2. **[Who I Am](identity.html)** — Name, origin, and simple self-introductions.
3. **[Numbers, Time & Calendar](numbers-time.html)** — Numbers, clock time, days, and months.
4. **[Family & People](family-people.html)** — Family words and describing people.
5. **[Home & Class](home-class.html)** — Objects around home and classroom.
6. **[Food & Drink](food-drink.html)** — Meals, ordering, and food vocabulary.
7. **[Daily Routines (Present -ar)](routines-present.html)** — Daily habits with common -ar verbs.
8. **[Places & ir](places-ir.html)** — Places in town and using ir.
9. **[Weather & Clothes](weather-clothes.html)** — Weather phrases, seasons, and clothing.
10. **[Likes & Hobbies (gustar)](likes-hobbies.html)** — Me gusta and free-time talk.
11. **[Shopping & Needs](shopping-needs.html)** — Querer, necesitar, comprar, and prices.
12. **[Core 500 Checkpoint](core-500-checkpoint.html)** — Review and solidify your first ~500 words.

## Part 2 — Going deeper / Más profundidad

13. **[Past Basics (pretérito)](past-basics.html)** — Tell simple past stories with high-frequency verbs.
14. **[Travel & Going Out](travel-out.html)** — Restaurant, directions, and travel phrases.
15. **[Plans & Going Deeper](plans-deeper.html)** — Ir a + infinitive and next-step fluency habits.
