# Learning Materials

This is the heart of the course: a step-by-step path distilled from Dr. Justin
Sung's videos (see [Sources](sources.html)). Lessons are listed in a
**suggested chronological order** — each builds on the last if you want a
default path. You can open any lesson at any time; you are not stuck on one
concept before the next.

When you are ready to practice, the **[Practice app](practice.html)** lets you
start any session in any subject and shows a calendar of the days you actually
spent learning.

## Part 1 — Get the foundations right

1. **[How Learning Really Works](the-science.html)** — studying vs. learning;
   retention + mastery; isolated vs. integrated knowledge; schemas;
   neuroplasticity.
2. **[Myths & the Misinterpreted-Effort Trap](myths-mindset.html)** — why "easy =
   effective" and "learning styles" hold you back; becoming the mechanic of your
   own brain.
3. **[The Effort–Time Exchange](effort-struggle.html)** — spend effort to buy back
   time; the generation effect; "how can I organize this?"

## Part 2 — Think at the right level

4. **[The 6 Levels of Thinking](higher-order-thinking.html)** — Bloom's levels and
   why you should study top-down (start at *evaluate*).

## Part 3 — Take it in well (encoding)

5. **[The PACER Method](pacer.html)** — sort information into five types; balance
   consumption with digestion.
6. **[Encoding: Build the Big Picture](encoding.html)** — simplify first, learn in
   layers, iterate with fast hypotheses.
7. **[Thinking on Paper](notes-on-paper.html)** — make it wrong, shorter, again;
   non-linear maps; intuitive vs. logical grouping.

## Part 4 — Make it stick (retrieval & revision)

8. **[Active Recall, Done Right](active-recall.html)** — free vs. cued vs.
   recognition; practice how you play.
9. **[Spacing & the Forgetting Curve](spacing.html)** — the spacing effect, and
   avoiding flashcard "learning debt."
10. **[Interleaving](interleaving.html)** — mix topics to train decision-making.

## Part 5 — Skills, systems & consistency

11. **[Learning Skills & Mental Models](theory-overload.html)** — avoid theory
    overload; balance theory with practice; think with models.
12. **[Motivation & Your Study System](motivation-systems.html)** — the DEFUSE
    method, a structured session, and the lessons that tie it all together.
